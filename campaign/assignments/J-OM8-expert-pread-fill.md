## Why
Over-memory decode (Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, 20.8 GiB on 7.4 GiB, `-ngl 99 -cmoe -lm mmap`) is NVMe-bound: ~56 GiB read per 64-token run at ~95 KiB/IO, 57% of bytes unused (default readahead). J-OM7 issued MADV_WILLNEED at the hook: zero lead time, so faults still read 4-5 KiB pages and decode fell 38%. The drive does 536 MiB/s at 576 KiB qd8 but 20 MiB/s at 4 KiB qd1. Hypothesis: make the needed expert bytes resident *synchronously and with large parallel reads* right at the hook, so the CPU matmul only takes minor faults.
