Here's a thinking process:

1.  **Analyze User Input:**
   - **Goal:** Reduce traffic near a school
   - **Requirements:** Suggest three practical changes
   - **For each change:** Explain one benefit and one tradeoff
   - **Context:** Town/school setting, traffic reduction

2.  **Deconstruct Requirements:**
   - Need 3 distinct, practical interventions
   - Each must have:
     - Clear description of the change
     - One specific benefit
     - One specific tradeoff
   - Keep it concise and practical

3.  **Brainstorming Potential Changes:**
   - Staggered school start/end times
   - Dedicated school zone traffic calming measures (speed bumps, crosswalks, signage)
   - Promoting/mandating carpooling or shuttle services
   - Implementing a "walking school bus" program
   - Restricting parent drop-off/pick-up zones and creating designated areas
   - Expanding public transit routes/schedules near school
   - Implementing a school traffic management plan with crossing guards/volunteers
   - Congestion pricing/tolling near school (probably not practical for a town/school context)
