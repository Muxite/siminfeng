Here's a thinking process that leads to the suggested traffic reduction strategies:

1.  **Analyze the Request:**
    *   **Goal:** Reduce traffic near a school.
    *   **Quantity:** Three practical changes.
    *   **Requirement:** For each change, explain one benefit and one tradeoff.
    *   **Context:** Urban planning/traffic management/school safety.

2.  **Brainstorming Potential Solutions:**
    *   *School Bus Routes:* Consolidate routes, use larger buses.
    *   *Drop-off/Pick-up Zones:* Stagger times, create specific zones.
    *   *Walking/Biking:* Safe routes, walking buses, bike racks.
    *   *Traffic Calming:* Speed bumps, chicanes, narrowed lanes.
    *   *Parking:* Restrict parking, move parents to a distance.
    *   *Technology:* App-based carpooling, traffic lights.
    *   *Zoning:* No entry during school hours.
