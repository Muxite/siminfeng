Here's a thinking process:

1.  **Analyze User Input:**
   - **Topic:** Why the sky looks blue
   - **Target Audience:** A curious child
   - **Requirements:** Short, clear explanation

2.  **Identify Key Scientific Concepts:**
   - Sunlight contains all colors (white light)
   - Earth's atmosphere has tiny particles/gases
   - Sunlight scatters when it hits these particles
   - Blue light scatters more than other colors (Rayleigh scattering)
   - This scattered blue light reaches our eyes from all directions

3.  **Translate to Child-Friendly Language:**
   - Avoid jargon like "Rayleigh scattering," "wavelengths," "atmospheric particles"
   - Use analogies or simple imagery
   - Keep it short and engaging
   - Focus on the core idea: sunlight + air = blue sky
