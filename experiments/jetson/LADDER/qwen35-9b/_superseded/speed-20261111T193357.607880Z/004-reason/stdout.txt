Here's a thinking process that leads to the suggested traffic reduction strategies:

1.  **Analyze the Request:**
    *   **Goal:** Reduce traffic near a school.
    *   **Quantity:** Three practical changes.
    *   **Requirement:** For each change, explain one benefit and one tradeoff.
    *   **Context:** A town setting (implies public infrastructure, community involvement, budget constraints).

2.  **Brainstorming Potential Solutions:**
    *   *School Start Times:* Later start times reduce morning rush.
    *   *Staggered Arrival:* Different times for different grades.
    *   *Carpooling:* Encourage ride-sharing.
    *   *Walking Buses:* Organized groups of kids walking.
    *   *Traffic Calming:* Speed bumps, chicanes, narrowed lanes.
    *   *Drop-off Zones:* Designated zones, timed entry.
    *   *Public Transport:* Shuttle buses.
    *   *Parking Restrictions:* No parking on school streets.

3.  **Selecting the Top Three (Practicality & Impact):**
    *   *Selection 1: Staggered Arrival Times.* Very
