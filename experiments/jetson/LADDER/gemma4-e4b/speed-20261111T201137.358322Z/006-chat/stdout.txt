Here's a thinking process that leads to the suggested explanation:

1.  **Analyze the Request:**
    *   **Topic:** Why the sky is blue.
    *   **Audience:** A curious child.
    *   **Style:** Short, clear, engaging (appropriate for a child).

2.  **Determine the Core Scientific Concept (The "Why"):**
    *   The sky is blue because of Rayleigh scattering.
    *   *Simplified:* Sunlight is made of many colors. The Earth's atmosphere (air) scatters these colors. Blue light scatters much more easily than other colors (like red or yellow).

3.  **Translate the Science into Child-Friendly Language (The "How"):**
    *   *Avoid:* "Rayleigh scattering," "wavelength," "electromagnetic spectrum."
    *   *Use:* Simple analogies, action words (bouncing, scattering, dancing).
    *   *Key elements to include:* Sunlight, air/atmosphere, colors, blue light.
