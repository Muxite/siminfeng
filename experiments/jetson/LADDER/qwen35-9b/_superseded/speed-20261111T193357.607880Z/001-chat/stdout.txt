Thinking Process:

1.  **Analyze the Request:**
    *   **Topic:** Why the sky looks blue.
    *   **Target Audience:** A curious child (needs simple language, engaging tone, relatable analogies).
    *   **Constraint:** Short, clear explanation.

2.  **Identify Key Scientific Concepts:**
    *   Sunlight is white (contains all colors).
    *   Earth's atmosphere has tiny particles (gas molecules).
    *   Scattering (Rayleigh scattering).
    *   Blue light scatters more than other colors.
    *   We see the scattered light.

3.  **Drafting - Attempt 1 (Mental Outline):**
    Sunlight has all colors. When it hits the air, the blue parts bounce around more. Red and green go straight. So we see blue everywhere.

4.  **Refining for a Child (Simplifying & Engaging):**
    *   *Analogy:* Like a prism or a game of dodgeball.
    *   *Language:* Avoid "Rayleigh scattering," "wavelengths," "atmosphere." Use "air," "tiny particles," "bouncing."
